Audio Ecosystem

Voice Channels

Section 04

Logo Usage Guidelines

Clear space boundaries, background contrast specifications, and minimum rendering sizes.

4.1 Clear Space Perimeter

Always preserve an exclusion zone around all sides of the logo equal to at least the height of the lowercase letter "e" in the Listune wordmark. No text, competing symbols, borders, or graphics may encroach within this boundary.

e e e e
Exclusion zone perimeter = height of lowercase letter "e"

4.2 Background Contrast Rules

  • Dark Backgrounds: Use the White wordmark or Pink wordmark.
  • Brand Pink Backgrounds: Always use the White wordmark. Never place pink logos on pink backgrounds.
  • Light and White Backgrounds: Use the Monochrome Black wordmark or Pink wordmark. Never place white logos on white backgrounds.
  • Complex Imagery: Place the wordmark over a dark translucent backdrop to guarantee legibility.

4.3 Minimum Display Dimensions

  • Digital Wordmark: Do not render smaller than 120 pixels wide.
  • Circular Symbol: Do not render smaller than 24 x 24 pixels on digital screens.
  • Always ensure the curved soundwave detail remains sharp and unblurred.

6.1 Logo Lettering Anatomy

The Listune wordmark uses custom geometric sans-serif lettering characterized by bold stroke weight, a clean vertical capital L, smooth terminal curves on lowercase t, and circular geometry on e, u, and n. It is custom lettering rather than a single stock font.

6.4 Typography Personality

  • Geometric & Confident: Strong, clear headlines with balanced tracking and comfortable leading.
  • Discord-Native: Modern and tech-centric, fitting naturally inside community environments.
Section 07

Visual Style and Atmosphere

The aesthetic atmosphere of Listune feels like a late-night music listening lounge: calm, dark, atmospheric, illuminated by vibrant pink accents.

Pillar 01

Dark-First Elegance

Listune lives in dark mode. Interfaces use deep charcoal and soft black foundations rather than stark neutral grays, creating a calm late-night listening environment.

Pillar 02

Electric Pink Focus

Vibrant Listune Pink (#f3678e) is applied intentionally to key action buttons, active playing states, and brand marks, guiding user attention without visual noise.

Pillar 03

Translucent Surfaces

Layered cards with subtle 1px border strokes (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)) convey depth, polish, and structure without heavy drop shadows or clutter.

Pillar 04

Rounded Geometry

Approachable rounded corners (14px on panels, pill shapes on status tags) produce a friendly, community-centric aesthetic that feels native to modern Discord apps.

Section 13

Brand Do and Don't Guidelines

Essential rules for maintaining visual consistency across community touchpoints, graphics, and integrations.

Do Logo & Identity
  • Use the white logo variant on dark backgrounds and navigation headers.
  • Use the circular symbol for profile pictures, avatars, and app icons.
  • Preserve the exact primary Listune Pink (#f3678e).
  • Keep the full clear space boundary around the emblem.
Do Visual Presentation
  • Maintain strong contrast between text and background surfaces.
  • Use rounded geometry for containers and interactive buttons.
  • Pair the vibrant pink accent with calm, deep dark backgrounds.
  • Keep card surfaces subtle with thin 1px border strokes.
Do Typography
  • Use Inter for body text, descriptions, and user interfaces.
  • Use Plus Jakarta Sans for display headlines and promotional titles.
  • Maintain comfortable line heights (1.5 to 1.6) for sustained reading.
  • Use bold weights (700/800) for strong visual hierarchy.
Don't Logo Violations
  • Do not distort, stretch, or alter the logo aspect ratio.
  • Do not rotate the logo or place it at tilted angles.
  • Do not recolor the logo with unauthorized rainbow or neon hues.
  • Do not add outer glows, heavy shadows, or bevel filters.
Don't Presentation Violations
  • Do not build light-gray or all-white interface layouts; Listune is dark-first.
  • Do not clutter banners with excessive decorative elements.
  • Do not use generic clip-art or random music emojis in place of brand assets.
  • Do not use low-contrast text that compromises legibility.
Don't Type Violations
  • Do not use ornate, gothic, or novelty fonts for brand communications.
  • Do not use inconsistent font weights across related marketing collateral.
  • Do not set body text below 14px on desktop interfaces.
  • Do not use artificial letter spacing on body paragraphs.
